# SEO + AEO Checklist for Founders

A practical, interactive checklist covering everything founders need to do to make their product discoverable — on Google, Bing, and AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

Most founders ship their product and assume traffic will follow. It doesn't. This checklist exists to fix that.

---

## What's Inside

**34 actionable items** across 5 phases:

1. **Foundation & Indexing** — Search Console, sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical tags
2. **On-Page & Technical SEO** — Titles, meta descriptions, Core Web Vitals, heading structure
3. **AI Engine Optimization (AEO)** — llms.txt, JSON-LD schema, FAQ markup, entity definition
4. **Content & Authority** — Pillar pages, comparison pages, backlinks, changelog
5. **Monitoring & Analytics** — GA4, Search Console habits, uptime, brand mentions

Each item includes a short note explaining *why* it matters, not just *what* to do.

---

## Why AEO Is Now Part of This

SEO used to mean Google rankings. That's still true — but it's no longer the whole picture.

AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot) are crawling the open web and citing products directly in their answers. If your site isn't structured for machine readability — proper metadata, `llms.txt`, schema markup, clear entity definitions — you're invisible to this entire discovery layer.

Founders who set this up early are seeing consistent AI-referred traffic. Founders who skip it are wondering why their competitors keep getting mentioned and they don't.

## How to Use It

Open `index.html` in any browser. Check off items as you complete them. Progress saves locally in your browser — no account needed.

Each item is tagged with a suggested timeline:

- `DAY 1` — Do this before or the day you launch
- `WEEK 1` — Critical first-week tasks
- `WEEK 2` — Second pass after basics are done
- `MONTH 1` — Content and authority building
- `ONGOING` — Recurring habits that compound over time
